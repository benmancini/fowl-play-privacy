Fowl Play Privacy Policy

Publisher: P R B S Gaming
Contact: ben.r.mancini@gmail.com
Effective date: 25 September 2026

Which versions this covers

Fowl Play is an Android game. Older builds through 0.1.19 have no advertising or in-app purchases. Newer builds include optional services described below; availability depends on the build, age profile and service setup. Test advertisements can also contact Google's services. You do not need a Fowl Play account to play.

Local progress and age choice

Scores, feathers, unlocked chickens and maps, cosmetics, missions, daily challenges, preferences and recovery copies are stored on your device. Your device date resets daily challenges. We do not operate a cloud-save service or receive your saved game progress.

The game asks whether you are under 18, 18 or older, or prefer not to say. It stores the resulting local profile, not your date of birth. Starting with build 0.1.23, under-18 and unknown-age profiles can receive non-personalised ads with child-directed treatment and a maximum G content rating. These profiles are not asked to consent to personalised ads, and the game's purchasing service is not started for them. Selecting an age group does not provide an ad-free entitlement. Earlier builds through 0.1.22 did not start advertising or purchasing services for these profiles. Android, Google Play and websites you open operate separately under their own policies.

Advertising and privacy choices

Google AdMob may provide advertisements between runs, with protections based on the age profile as described above. Its services process IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), app and ad interactions, performance and diagnostic information, and permitted device or account identifiers to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Fowl Play removes the Android advertising-ID permission and does not request precise device location; other service identifiers may still be processed. Non-personalised advertising still involves service data processing.

Google's User Messaging Platform checks whether a privacy message is needed. Where required, it asks for your choices before ads can be requested. Available choices can be reopened through Privacy & Ads > Ad Privacy Choices. Depending on your choices and applicable rules, limited or non-personalised ads may still be available. If the required privacy check fails, the game remains playable without starting ads.

See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner apps.

Purchases and Unity services

Where available, adults can buy a one-time Remove Ads entitlement through Google Play using Unity In-App Purchasing. Google handles payment and associates the purchase with your Google account. The game receives product and transaction or receipt information to grant and restore the entitlement and caches ownership locally. We do not receive your card details through the game. Restore Purchases checks ownership with Google Play; refunds or revocations may change it.

The installed Unity purchasing SDK can process installation and session identifiers, device and app information, country or locale, purchase events and diagnostic information to operate, secure and improve the purchasing service. This is separate from your locally saved game progress. The game does not ask you to create a Unity account or supply an email address for an in-app purchase.

See Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and Unity's purchasing privacy information.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Local data remains until you clear the game's storage or it is otherwise removed. In Android Settings, select Fowl Play and clear its storage to remove progress, age preferences and recovery copies. This does not cancel a Google Play purchase. Android backup and restore depend on your device and Google account settings.

Google and Unity retain service and transaction records according to their policies and applicable obligations. Contact us for access, correction or deletion requests concerning data processed for Fowl Play, including purchase-service data processed on our behalf. We will work with the relevant provider where needed. For data a provider controls independently, you can also use that provider's privacy controls. Some purchase or security records may need to be retained after a deletion request.

Support emails

If you email us, P R B S Gaming receives your email address and the information you provide. We use Gmail to respond to your enquiry. We retain correspondence for up to 12 months after resolution, unless a specific legal requirement or ongoing dispute requires longer. You can request deletion at ben.r.mancini@gmail.com.

This website and updates

GitHub Pages hosts this policy. Visiting it opens a website outside the game. GitHub may process technical request information such as your IP address under its privacy statement.

We will update this policy when the game's data practices change. For privacy questions, contact P R B S Gaming at ben.r.mancini@gmail.com.
