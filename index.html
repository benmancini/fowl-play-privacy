Fowl Play Privacy Policy

Publisher: P R B S Gaming
Contact: ben.r.mancini@gmail.com
Effective date: 21 September 2026

The Android game

Fowl Play does not collect or share personal data through the game. It has no player accounts, advertising or in-app payments. The current Android release works offline and does not request internet permission. Analytics and diagnostic reporting are disabled.

Local progress

Scores, feathers, unlocked chickens and maps, cosmetics, mission and daily challenge progress, audio preferences and recovery copies are stored on your device. Your device date resets daily challenges. We do not receive this information or provide a cloud-save service.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you clear the game's storage or it is otherwise removed. To delete it, use Android Settings, select Fowl Play, then clear its storage. This resets progress and recovery copies. Android backup and restore depend on your device and Google account settings and are controlled separately from the game.

Support emails

If you email ben.r.mancini@gmail.com, P R B S Gaming receives your email address and the information you include. We use it to respond to your enquiry. Support is handled through Gmail. We retain correspondence for up to 12 months after resolution, then delete it unless a specific legal requirement or ongoing dispute requires longer retention. You can request deletion at the same email address.

This website and external services

This page is hosted by GitHub Pages. Visiting it opens a website outside the game. GitHub may process technical request information, such as your IP address, under its privacy statement. Google Play, Android backup services and Gmail operate under their respective privacy policies.

Updates and contact

We will update this policy if the game's data practices change. For privacy questions or support-data deletion requests, contact P R B S Gaming at ben.r.mancini@gmail.com.
